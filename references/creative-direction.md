# Creative Direction

Understand product category, audience, goals, brand personality, content, and technical constraints before choosing aesthetics. Write a concise art-direction brief: concept, emotional tone, hierarchy, composition, typography, color roles, imagery, and motion.

Avoid defaulting to purple gradients, glass cards everywhere, generic centered hero sections, repetitive feature cards, decorative blobs, random icons, fake testimonials, fake metrics, or unrelated stock images. Use asymmetry, editorial layouts, bento grids, restrained minimalism, or expressive typography only when they fit the product.

Prefer one coherent visual idea executed carefully over unrelated effects. Preserve established brand identity unless redesign is requested.
