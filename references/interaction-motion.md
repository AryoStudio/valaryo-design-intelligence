# Interaction & Motion

Define relevant default, hover, focus-visible, pressed, active, disabled, loading, success, and error states. Do not rely on color alone.

Motion should explain cause/effect, provide feedback, preserve context, or clarify hierarchy. Avoid decorative movement that competes with content. Support mouse, keyboard, touch, and assistive technology. Never make essential actions hover-only. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and avoid expensive animation where possible.
